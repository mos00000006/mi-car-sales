# MiCarSales CRM – Updated Features

- Added a Staff CRM button to the public dealership navigation.
- CRM login remains protected by Supabase authentication.
- CRM styling updated to MiCarSales blue with yellow/gold accents.
- Manager-only Staff & Performance page.
- Manager can see salesperson names, roles, phone numbers, active status, cars sold and sales value.
- Manager can see recent sales with salesperson, customer, vehicle and sale price.
- Managers can choose which salesperson a recorded sale belongs to.
- Salespeople continue to have their own CRM access and sales are attributed to their profile.
